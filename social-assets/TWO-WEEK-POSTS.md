### Thursday — `w2-06-feed-colour-care.png`

> Three things that make your colour last, and none of them cost anything.
>
> 1. Wait 72 hours before the first wash. The cuticle is still closing — washing early is what
> pulls your toner straight back out.
> 2. Turn the water down. Hot water opens the cuticle every single wash.
> 3. Heat protectant every time. Not most times.
>
> Do these three and you'll genuinely stretch weeks out of a colour.
>
> #haircare #colouredhair #camberwellhair #melbournecolourist #hairtips
> #melbournehairdresser #hairbysha

**Why:** saveable and shareable, which is what Instagram rewards. It also protects your work
and quietly makes the case that you know what you're doing.

## Posting notes

**Best times for a Camberwell audience:** weekday mornings 7–9am, or evenings 7–9pm. Saturdays
work late morning.

**Reels beat static** for local discovery. If Sha can film even 10 seconds of the colour being
applied, put that in front of any of these as a Reel and use the graphic as the cover frame.

**Reply to every comment within an hour or two** where possible. Early engagement is the
strongest signal you can send Instagram, and for a local business the comments *are* the
sales conversation.

**Two of the eight are templates** — the before/afters need a real "before" photo dropped into
the left panel. Everything else is ready to post as-is.

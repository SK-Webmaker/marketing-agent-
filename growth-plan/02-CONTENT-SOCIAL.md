## Strategic position

You are not competing for reach. You are a **one-chair colour specialist with finite capacity** — roughly 6–8 appointments a week on the new Thursday/Friday availability alone. You do not need a viral account. You need *enough of the right people in Camberwell to know you exist and to trust your colour work before they book.*

That changes the content brief completely:

- **Reach is a vanity metric here.** 500 local followers who need grey blending beats 50,000 nationally.
- **Proof beats personality.** Colour clients are risk-averse. They have usually been burned. The content job is de-risking, not entertaining.
- **The content must survive the referral moment.** When a client tells a friend "book with Sha", the friend looks her up. If the last post is four months old, the referral dies there. **This is the single most important function of the account** — it backstops every other channel.

## Format mix

| Format | Share | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Before / after transformations** | 40% | The core proof asset. Colour clients buy on evidence. |
| **Process / craft** (foiling, toning, K18 application) | 20% | Justifies premium pricing, shows the 20 years |
| **Educating on the problem** (brassiness, grown-out colour, box dye) | 20% | Captures search intent, positions expertise |
| **Sha on camera** (face, voice, opinions) | 15% | One-chair salons sell the person. Highest-trust format. |
| **Salon / atmosphere / availability** | 5% | Reinforces the calm one-to-one differentiator |

**Do not post graphics-only content.** Text-on-background posts underperform badly for salons and read as filler. Every post should show hair, hands, or Sha.

## Cadence — realistic for a solo operator

| Platform | Frequency | Reality check |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram feed** | 3× per week | Tue / Thu / Sat |
| **Instagram Stories** | 4–5× per week | Behind-the-scenes, low effort, 15 seconds |
| **TikTok** | 2× per week | Repurpose the same vertical footage |
| **Reels** | 2 of the 3 weekly feed posts | Reels still out-reach static for local discovery |

**Capture discipline is the whole game.** Sha cannot stop mid-foil to plan content. The system has to be: phone on a tripod, film every colour service start-to-finish, edit once a week in a single 45-minute block. One shooting habit feeds every platform.

### Repurposing rule
Every Reel → TikTok same week, caption rewritten for TikTok's flatter, less polished tone. Every before/after → Story with a poll sticker. One post per month → Google Business Profile post (see Pillar 1 — this directly feeds local SEO).

## Caption framework

Salon captions fail by being about the salon. Use: **problem → what I did → what it means for you → CTA.**

Real example, ready to use:

> Six months of grown-out box dye and a lot of "I've just given up on it."
>
> We went dimensional rather than flat — foils through the top, a gloss to knock back the warmth, and K18 through the mid-lengths because the ends had had enough.
>
> The point of blending grey rather than chasing it: your regrowth stops being a deadline. You get eight to ten weeks instead of four.
>
> Colour appointments open Thursdays and Fridays. Link in bio, or text me on 0452 611 799.

Regulars

Rebook without being asked. Protect them. Never discount them.

Driftors

The opportunity. They do come back, just late. Shortening their gap is where the 99 comes from.

Lapsed

Not seen 6+ months. Win-back is parked until Kairo has the history to identify them.

New / referred

Habit not formed. Get them to visit three and they become Regulars.

Four touches. The first is worth more than the other three combined.

01The ask, in the chair

Two named times before they reach the counter. Never “when suits you?”
